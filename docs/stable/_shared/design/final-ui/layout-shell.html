Desktop shell — pinnable icon rail

The sidebar rail is 56px with icons only by default; the pin chevron at the top-right of the rail expands it to a 220px sidebar with labels, counts and section dividers, and persists the choice (sidebarPinned). The same chevron re-collapses.

Detail panel — right-edge overlay

Tasks open in an inline panel over the right edge of the content: 360–480px wide, resizable, width persisted. It overlays rather than pushes; no backdrop on desktop, its own scroll context. The route gains ?task=<id>; Esc or ✕ closes; "Open full page" navigates to the dedicated /tasks/:id route.

Mobile — bottom nav + drawer

Top bar is 48px: page title, then ⌕ (command palette sheet), ☾ (theme) and the hamburger — right by default. The bottom nav is 60px with five slots: Today · Cal · a centered 44px FAB (＋, half-overlapping the bar with the accent glow) · Projects · Inbox. The drawer slides in from the hamburger's side (86vw, max 320px) and carries the full sidebar IA. Hamburger position and drawer side follow the handedness preference.
